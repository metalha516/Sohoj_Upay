# Sohoj Voice & Tone Guidelines

When authoring copy, UI text, AI coach responses, or documentation for Sohoj, strictly adhere to this persona.

---

## 1. Core Persona Attributes
* **Empowering & Non-Judgmental:** Personal finance in emerging markets carries anxiety. Advice should celebrate discipline and encourage incremental savings rather than scolding overspending.
* **Culturally Grounded:** Understand Bangladesh's financial reality (Eid expenses, seasonal gifts, family support, informal lending, DPS/FDR laddering).
* **Mathematically Honest:** Never claim guaranteed returns. Always include disclaimers on compound growth simulations and forecasts.
* **Bilingual Fluency:** Seamlessly bridge Bengali financial idioms (টাকা, সঞ্চয়, ডিপিএস) and technical financial terminology.

---

## 2. Terminology Standards

| Do Not Use | Use Instead | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "BDT 500" / "Tk 500" | "৳500" | Always use the standard Taka symbol |
| "ATM Withdrawal" | "Cash Out" (ক্যাশ আউট) | MFS standard terminology |
| "Transfer" | "Send Money" (সেন্ড মানি) | MFS standard terminology |
| "Guaranteed Profit" | "Projected Return / Historical Yield" | Compliance & financial safety |
| "Utilize" | "Use" | Clarity & plain English |
| "In order to" | "To" | Concise writing |

---

## 3. UI Microcopy Guidelines
* **Headings:** Title Case for main headers (H1, H2). Sentence case for descriptions and subheaders.
* **Alerts:** Clear, actionable, and non-alarmist. For unusual cash-outs: *"We noticed a higher-than-usual Cash Out of ৳15,000. Would you like to tag its purpose?"*
* **Numbers:** Format large amounts with commas (`৳1,50,000` or `৳150,000`).
