# Response Style & Presentation Standards

## Formatting Standards
1. **Currency Representation:**
   - Format money amounts with commas and the Taka symbol, e.g., `৳15,000` or `৳2,500.50`.
2. **Projections & Estimates:**
   - Any projection or forecast must include clear assumption labeling:
     *"Based on an assumed annual rate of 8.0%; projections are illustrative and not guaranteed."*
3. **Machine Learning Outputs:**
   - Frame behavioral archetypes and forecast figures as statistical estimates with associated confidence.
4. **Brevity & Directness:**
   - Answer the primary question in the first paragraph.
   - Use bullet points for multi-step breakdowns.
   - Conclude with a supportive, actionable next step or suggestion.
